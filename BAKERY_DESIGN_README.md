# Artisan Bakery Interior Design

## Overview

This directory contains a complete interior design solution for an artisan bakery with Middle Eastern heritage influences. The design addresses all specified requirements for an 11m × 5m space with rustic modern aesthetics.

## Design Requirements Met

✅ **Space Dimensions**: 11 meters long × 5 meters wide (55m² total area)  
✅ **Orientation**: North-facing glass storefront  
✅ **Style**: Rustic modern bakery with Middle Eastern heritage  
✅ **Open Stone Oven**: Visible to customers on the right side  
✅ **Bread Display**: Located on the left side  
✅ **Clear Central Walking Path**: 1.2m wide corridor  
✅ **Cashier Counter**: Positioned after the display area  
✅ **Preparation Area**: At the back (staff only)  
✅ **Materials**: Warm lighting, natural wood, stone, brick  
✅ **Atmosphere**: Cozy, authentic, non-corporate

## Design Highlights

### Spatial Layout (North to South)

1. **North Section - Entrance Zone**
   - Full-width glass storefront for natural light
   - Central entrance door (1.2m wide)
   - Clear visibility of interior and stone oven from outside

2. **Left Side - Bread Display Area**
   - 1.5m deep × 3.0m long shelving unit
   - 4-tier reclaimed wood shelving
   - Woven baskets and tilted display
   - Immediate visibility upon entry

3. **Right Side - Open Stone Oven**
   - 1.6m × 2.5m visible cooking area
   - Natural stone and exposed brick
   - Arched opening with visible flames
   - Fully visible to customers in walking path

4. **Center - Walking Path**
   - 1.2m wide × 9.0m long clear corridor
   - Wide-plank natural oak flooring
   - Enables customer flow and oven viewing

5. **Cashier Counter**
   - 1.5m wide × 1.0m deep
   - Butcher block wood top with copper accents
   - Positioned after display area
   - Integrated POS system

6. **Back Section - Preparation Area**
   - Southern end (3.0m deep)
   - Staff-only workspace
   - Stainless steel work surfaces
   - Storage and mixing stations

### Materials & Finishes

**Flooring**
- Main Area: Wide-plank natural oak (matte finish)
- Prep Area: Sealed concrete with wood accents

**Walls**
- Primary: Exposed brick in warm earth tones
- Accent: White lime wash plaster
- Feature: Reclaimed wood paneling

**Oven Area**
- Stone: Natural fieldstone or limestone
- Brick: Terracotta red with visible mortar
- Hearth: Heat-resistant clay tiles

**Counters & Display**
- Display Shelves: Reclaimed wood with natural grain
- Cashier Counter: Butcher block maple or oak
- Prep Surfaces: Stainless steel with wood edges

**Accents**
- Metal: Brushed copper and bronze fixtures
- Textiles: Woven baskets, linen bread bags
- Details: Hand-forged iron brackets, ceramic tiles

### Lighting Design

**Ambient Lighting**
- 4 copper/brass pendant lights strategically placed
- Warm white temperature (2700-3000K)
- Industrial-artisan style with Edison bulbs

**Task Lighting**
- LED strips under display shelves
- Focused pendants over cashier station
- Natural firelight from stone oven

**Natural Light**
- North-facing storefront for maximum daylight
- Soft, even light quality
- Linen curtains/shades for evening ambiance control

## Design Philosophy

This artisan bakery design celebrates traditional baking heritage while providing modern comfort and functionality. The space creates an authentic experience through:

- **Visible Craftsmanship**: Open oven, handmade displays
- **Natural Materials**: Honest use of wood, stone, brick
- **Warm Atmosphere**: Lighting, colors, textures
- **Cultural Authenticity**: Middle Eastern heritage elements
- **Customer Engagement**: Visible baking process
- **Clear Flow**: Intuitive entrance to exit journey

The design balances rustic charm with modern efficiency, creating a non-corporate, community-focused bakery that emphasizes quality, tradition, and genuine artisan craft.

## Customer Experience Journey

1. **Approach**: Glass storefront creates anticipation and view of oven
2. **Entry**: Aroma of fresh bread greets at door
3. **Browse**: Immediate view of bread display on left
4. **Engage**: Watch baking process through oven viewing
5. **Purchase**: Clear path to cashier counter
6. **Exit**: Satisfied, with clear route out
